Our Story

Born to Dress
Different

ZORO Wear started as a vision — to bring international-quality streetwear to every corner of India, without the international price tag.

Who We Are

Premium Streetwear,
Proudly Indian

ZORO Wear was founded in Chennimalai, Tamil Nadu — a small town with big dreams. We believed that young India deserves fashion that doesn't ask them to choose between quality and affordability.

Every piece we create is a statement. From the weight of the fabric to the precision of the cut, we obsess over the details so you can obsess over your look.

Today, ZORO Wear ships to thousands of customers across India, bringing premium oversized tees, jerseys, and premium bottoms to every city, town, and village.

Our Journey

2025

The Spark

ZORO Wear was born from a garage in Chennimalai. The founder hand-picked fabrics from local mills and began crafting the first collection of 10 oversized tees.

2025

First Drop

Launched officially on WhatsApp with our debut collection. Sold out within a week. The overwhelming response confirmed there was a real demand for premium streetwear at honest prices.

2026

Expanding the Line

Added Football Jerseys, Collar T-Shirts, Track Pants and Shorts. Grew to over 2,000 customers and launched our first dedicated product website and Instagram page.

2026

National Presence

Now shipping pan-India with 5,000+ happy customers, a full product catalogue, and exciting new collections planned every season. This is just the beginning.
